## Pitch Deck (12–14 slides)

1) **Title / One-liner**  
- Company, logo, founder names  
- One-sentence value prop: *[Company] helps [customer] achieve [outcome] by [how]*

2) **Problem**  
- 2–4 bullets on pain (who, frequency, cost)  
- 1–2 crisp evidence points (metric or story)  

3) **Solution (High-level)**  
- 3–5 outcome bullets (avoid features)  
- Simple diagram: *problem → your system → measurable output*  
- “Today vs Next” roadmap (1 line)

4) **Why Now**  
- 2–3 tailwinds (tech/regulatory/buyer behavior/distribution shifts)  
- Why incumbents can’t respond fast

5) **Product / Workflow**  
- 1–2 screenshots or workflow mock  
- 3 core capabilities tied directly to the problem

6) **Market (TAM/SAM/SOM + wedge)**  
- Market sizing ranges + method placeholders  
- Your wedge segment (ICP) and expansion path

7) **Traction (Proof + momentum)**  
- Pick the best 4–8: ARR/revenue & growth, customers, retention/churn/cohorts, usage, pipeline, conversion, LOIs/pilot→paid, notable logos

8) **Business Model**  
- Pricing model (seat/usage/subscription tiers)  
- Directional ACV/ARPA, gross margin direction  
- Sales motion (PLG vs sales-led) at a glance

9) **Go-to-Market (brief but specific)**  
- ICP definition  
- Channels working today + funnel metrics placeholders  
- How you’ll scale distribution (2–3 bullets)

10) **Competition / Differentiation (“Why you”)**  
- Table: incumbents/alternatives + why they lose  
- 3–5 differentiation points mapped to buyer pain  
- Defensibility/moat type (data/workflow lock-in/network effects/etc.)

11) **Moat + Why This Team Wins**  
- 2–3 moat pillars  
- Compounding mechanism  
- Key risks + mitigations (light touch)

12) **Team**  
- 4–6 bullets: roles + prior wins  
- Advisors only if truly relevant  
- “Why we can execute” line

13) **The Ask (Funding request + use of funds)**  
- Round type and **$X** (placeholder)  
- Use of funds (3–6 buckets with %): product, GTM, hiring, ops/partnerships/compliance, runway/milestones  
- Milestones tied to spend (next 12–18 months)

14) **Financials / Key metrics dashboard (lean)**  
- 3-year summary (ranges + assumptions): revenue/ARR, growth, burn/runway  
- Directional CAC/payback, LTV/retention, churn  
- “If assumptions hold, what changes” (1 bullet)

*(Optional: If you’re later-stage and need it, you can add a governance/board/rights slide—otherwise keep the deck to 12–14.)*
