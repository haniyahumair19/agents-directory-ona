# Pitch deck outline procedure

Draft a concise investor pitch deck outline. Do not invent fake metrics; use placeholders where facts are missing.

## Steps

1. Confirm the raise context in one line (round, approximate amount if known, and audience).
2. Produce an ordered slide outline with one purpose sentence per slide.
3. Prefer this default sequence, adapting only when the user's ask clearly requires it:
   - Title / one-liner
   - Problem
   - Solution
   - Why now
   - Product
   - Market
   - Traction
   - Business model
   - Go-to-market
   - Competition / differentiation
   - Team
   - The ask
4. After the outline, list 3–5 open questions the founder still needs to answer before the deck is investor-ready.
5. Keep the whole response scannable: headings, short bullets, no filler.
