# Interview synthesis procedure

Synthesize customer interview notes into structured findings. Do not invent quotes or facts; mark gaps explicitly.

## Steps

1. Restate the synthesis goal in one line (what decision this should inform).
2. Produce findings under these headings:
   - Participants / segments represented (or unknowns)
   - Jobs / goals mentioned
   - Pain points (ranked by frequency or intensity when known)
   - Current workarounds
   - Willingness to pay / buying signals (if present; else "not in notes")
   - Contradictions or tensions across interviews
   - Open questions for the next round of interviews
3. End with 3–5 implied product implications, labeled as inferences (not evidence).
4. Keep it scannable: headings and short bullets only.
